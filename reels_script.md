🎬 TITLE: Ek Tarfa Pyaar - Woh Ankahee Kahani
🎯 HOOK: Kya kabhi aisa hua hai ki dil ne shor kiya ho, par zubaan khamosh rahi? Jab pyaar sirf ek tarfa ho...
📖 FULL STORY: Shehar ki bheed mein, woh roz dikhti thi. Ek haseen khwaab jaisi. Uska muskurana meri subah thi, uski khamoshi meri shaam. Maine kabhi usse kaha nahi, par meri nazrein har roz ek poori dastaan likhti thi. Yeh ek tarfa pyaar bhi ajeeb hota hai na, jaan? Ismein intezaar ki khubsurati hai, aur dard ki gehrai bhi. Jaise bin mausam barsaat, jo dil ko bhigo de par zameen tak na pahuche. Maine uski khushi mein apni duniya basayi, uski aankhon mein apne sapne dekhe. Aur shayad, yehi meri kahani ka sabse khubsurat sach tha. Ke kuch rishte manzil tak nahi, sirf ehsaas tak hote hain.
